## Ordering & Purchases

### How do I place an order?

**Steps:**
1. Browse products by niche or use search
2. Add items to your cart
3. Click "Cart" in header
4. Review items and enter shipping address
5. Select shipping method
6. Choose payment method
7. Review order and click "Place Order"
8. Confirmation email sent immediately

**Tips:**
- Create an account for faster checkout
- Save addresses for future purchases
- Check inventory before checkout

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:
- **Credit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **PayPal**: Fast and secure
- **Debit Cards**: Most major banks

All payments processed securely through encrypted gateways.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

**If order hasn't shipped (within 2 hours):**
- Email: support@zyro-electric.com
- Subject: "Cancel Order [Order Number]"
- Include: Order number and reason
- Refund issued within 24 hours

**If order already shipped:**
- Cannot cancel, but can return after delivery
- See [Return Policy](./RETURN_POLICY.md)

### Is my payment information secure?

Yes! We use:
- **SSL/TLS Encryption**: Data encrypted during transmission
- **PCI DSS Compliance**: Payment Card Industry standards
- **Tokenization**: Card numbers never stored
- **Fraud Detection**: Real-time monitoring
- **Secure Processors**: Stripe & PayPal

See [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for details.

## Shipping & Delivery

### How long does shipping take?

**Standard Shipping:**
- Processing: 2-5 business days
- Transit: 5-10 business days
- Total: 7-15 business days

**Expedited Shipping:**
- Processing: 1-2 business days
- Transit: 2-3 business days
- Total: 3-5 business days

**Note:** Excludes weekends and holidays. Processing starts after payment confirmed.

### Do you offer free shipping?

**Free Shipping:**
- Orders over $50 (continental US)
- Prime members (any order)
- Replacement items (defective/wrong)

**Paid Shipping:**
- Orders under $50: $5.99 - $9.99
- International: $15.99 - $49.99
- Shipping costs shown before checkout

### Can I track my order?

Yes! Track your order:

**Method 1: Email**
- Click "Track Order" in order confirmation email
- Enter order number and email

**Method 2: Your Account**
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Order History"
3. Find your order
4. Click "Track Shipment"
5. Real-time tracking updates

**Method 3: Carrier**
- Use tracking number with UPS, FedEx, or USPS website

### What if my package doesn't arrive?

**Report Within 7 Days of Delivery Date:**

1. Check tracking (may still be in transit)
2. Email: support@zyro-electric.com
3. Include: Order number, tracking number, delivery date

**We Will:**
- File carrier claim
- Investigate with shipping partner
- Replace item or issue refund
- Update you within 48 hours

## Returns & Refunds

### What is your return policy?

**30-Day Returns** for most items:
- Unopened, unused condition
- Original packaging intact
- No visible damage
- With order receipt

**Exceptions:**
- Clearance items (non-returnable)
- Custom items (non-returnable)
- Digital products (non-returnable)
- Items returned after 30 days

**See complete [Return Policy](./RETURN_POLICY.md)**

### How do I return an item?

**Online (Easiest):**
1. Log into account
2. Go to "Order History"
3. Find order
4. Click "Return Item"
5. Select reason and submit
6. Print return label
7. Ship item
8. Refund processed within 5-7 days

**By Email:**
- Email: returns@zyro-electric.com
- Include: Order number, reason for return

### When will I get my refund?

**Timeline:**
1. Item arrives at warehouse: 1-2 days
2. Inspection: 2-3 days
3. Approval: 1 day
4. Refund processed: 5-7 business days
5. Appears in account: 3-5 business days

**Total: 10-21 business days**

Refunds go to original payment method.

### Can I exchange an item?

Yes! Instead of returning for refund:

1. Select "Exchange" instead of "Return"
2. Choose replacement item/size/color
3. New item ships free
4. Return original when ready
5. Exchange complete

**No additional charge** if exchanging for same-price item.

## Account & Privacy

### How do I create an account?

**Quick Sign-Up:**
1. Click "Sign Up" in header
2. Enter email address
3. Create password
4. Enter name and phone (optional)
5. Click "Create Account"
6. Verify email (link sent)
7. Account active!

**Benefits:**
- Faster checkout
- Order history
- Saved addresses
- Wishlist
- Exclusive offers

### How do I reset my password?

**Forgot Password:**
1. Go to Login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter email address
4. Click "Send Reset Link"
5. Check email for reset link
6. Click link in email
7. Enter new password
8. Password updated!

**Link expires in 24 hours.**

### How is my data protected?

We protect your data with:
- **Encryption**: SSL/TLS for transmission
- **Secure Storage**: Encrypted database
- **Access Controls**: Limited employee access
- **Compliance**: GDPR, CCPA, PCI DSS
- **Privacy Policy**: See [full policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md)

Your data is never sold to third parties.

### Can I delete my account?

Yes! Delete your account:

1. Log into account
2. Go to "Settings"
3. Click "Delete Account"
4. Enter password to confirm
5. Account deleted
6. Confirmation email sent

**What happens:**
- Profile data deleted
- Order history retained (for tax/legal)
- Cannot recover account
- Can create new account anytime
