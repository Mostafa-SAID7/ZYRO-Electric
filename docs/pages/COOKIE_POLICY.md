# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 11, 2026

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, phone) when you visit our Platform. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences. Cookies help us enhance your experience and understand how you use our Platform.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the Platform to function properly:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Session ID | Maintains your login session | Session |
| CSRF Token | Prevents cross-site attacks | Session |
| Preferences | Saves your language/settings | 1 year |
| Cart Items | Stores shopping cart data | Session/persistent |

**You cannot disable essential cookies** - the Platform won't work without them.

### 2.2 Performance Cookies

These help us understand how you use the Platform:

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| _ga | Google Analytics | Tracks page views and user sessions |
| _gid | Google Analytics | Identifies unique visitors |
| _gat | Google Analytics | Throttles request rate |
| track_id | ZYRO | Tracks user behavior |
| page_views | ZYRO | Counts page visits |

**Purpose:** Improve Platform performance and user experience  
**Duration:** 2 years for analytics data

### 2.3 Marketing & Advertising Cookies

These enable personalized ads and marketing:

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| fbp | Facebook | Facebook pixel tracking |
| tr | Facebook | Conversion tracking |
| _fbp | Facebook | User identification |
| uet | Microsoft | Bing ads tracking |
| sc_is_visitor_unique | Snapchat | Snapchat analytics |

**Purpose:** Show relevant ads, track conversions, retarget users  
**Duration:** 1-3 years

### 2.4 Functional Cookies

These enhance your user experience:

| Cookie | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| lang | Your preferred language |
| timezone | Your timezone |
| theme | Light/dark mode preference |
| accessibility | Accessibility settings |

**Purpose:** Remember your preferences  
**Duration:** 1 year

## 4. Cookie Consent Management

### 4.1 Cookie Banner
When you first visit our Platform, a cookie banner appears explaining our cookie use. You can:

- **Accept All**: Allow all cookies
- **Reject All**: Disable non-essential cookies
- **Customize**: Choose which cookies to accept

### 4.2 Managing Your Preferences

You can change cookie preferences anytime:

1. Go to your account settings
2. Click "Cookie Preferences"
3. Toggle cookies on/off
4. Click "Save Preferences"

Your choices are stored and respected on future visits.

### 4.3 Browser Controls

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

**Chrome:**
- Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

**Firefox:**
- Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

**Safari:**
- Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

**Edge:**
- Settings → Privacy → Cookies and other site data

---

## 5. Do Not Track (DNT)

Your browser may have a "Do Not Track" feature. We recognize DNT signals, but we cannot guarantee third-party services do. When DNT is enabled:
- We will not use performance or marketing cookies
- Essential and functional cookies remain active
- You may have a reduced personalized experience

---

## 6. Data Retention

| Cookie Type | Retention Period |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Session Cookies | Until browser closes |
| Essential Cookies | 1-2 years |
| Performance Cookies | 2 years |
| Marketing Cookies | 1-3 years |
| Functional Cookies | 1 year |

After retention period expires, cookies are automatically deleted.

## 7. Cookie-Related Technologies

Beyond cookies, we use similar technologies:

### 7.1 Pixels (Web Beacons)
Small 1x1 images that track:
- Page views
- Link clicks
- Form submissions
- Email opens (in campaigns)

### 7.2 Local Storage
Stores data on your device:
- Shopping cart items
- User preferences
- Temporary form data
- Offline browsing data

### 7.3 Session Storage
Stores temporary data during your session:
- Current page state
- Form progress
- Temporary preferences

### 7.4 IndexedDB
Database-like storage for large amounts of data:
- Offline content
- Cached data
- Application data
