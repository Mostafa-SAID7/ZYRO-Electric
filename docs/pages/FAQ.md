# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Quick Answers to Common Questions**

---

## 🛒 Ordering & Shopping

**Q: How do I create an account?**  
A: Click "Sign Up" in the header, enter your email, create a password, and verify your email. Takes 2 minutes!

**Q: Can I shop without an account?**  
A: Yes, you can check out as a guest, but an account lets you track orders and save addresses.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Absolutely! We use SSL encryption and PCI DSS compliance. Your card data is never stored on our servers.

**Q: Can I use multiple coupon codes?**  
A: No, only one coupon code per order. The system will automatically apply the best discount.

**Q: Do you offer a loyalty program?**  
A: Yes! Sign up for our rewards program to earn points on every purchase.

## 📦 Shipping & Delivery

**Q: How much does shipping cost?**  
A: Standard shipping is $5.99 (free on orders over $50). Expedited shipping is $14.99. Overnight is $24.99.

**Q: How long does shipping take?**  
A: Standard: 7-15 business days. Expedited: 3-5 business days. Overnight: 1-2 business days.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**  
A: Yes! We ship to 150+ countries. Costs vary by location ($15.99-$49.99). Check at checkout.

**Q: Can I track my order?**  
A: Yes! You'll receive a tracking number via email when your order ships. Track it on our site or the carrier's website.

**Q: What if my package doesn't arrive?**  
A: Contact our support team within 7 days. We'll file a claim with the carrier or send a replacement.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?**  
A: Only if the order hasn't shipped yet (within 24 hours). Email support immediately with the new address.

## 🔄 Returns & Refunds

**Q: What's your return policy?**  
A: 30-day returns for most items. Must be unopened, unused, in original packaging. [See full policy](./RETURN_POLICY.md)

**Q: How do I return an item?**  
A: Log into your account → Order History → Find order → Click "Return Item" → Print label → Ship back.

**Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?**  
A: No! We cover return shipping for defective items or wrong items. For change of mind, you pay (usually $5-15).

**Q: When will I get my refund?**  
A: 10-21 business days from delivery. We inspect (2-3 days), approve (1 day), process (5-7 days), then it appears in your account (3-5 days).

**Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?**  
A: Yes! Select "Exchange" during return process. New item ships free while we receive the original.

**Q: What if an item arrives damaged?**  
A: Report within 48 hours with photos. We'll replace it or refund you—no return needed.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Go to login page → Click "Forgot Password?" → Enter email → Click link in email → Set new password.

**Q: How do I update my profile information?**

**Q: Is my personal data safe?**

**Q: Do you sell my data to third parties?**  
A: Absolutely not! Your data is used only to provide our service and improve your experience.
