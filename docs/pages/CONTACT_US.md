## 💬 FAQ

**Q: What's the best way to contact you?**  
A: Email for detailed issues, phone for urgent help, live chat for quick questions.

**Q: How quickly will I get a response?**  
A: Usually within 24 hours. Phone support is immediate during business hours.

**Q: Do you have weekend support?**  
A: No, but you can email anytime. We respond Monday-Friday.

**Q: What if I don't get a response?**  
A: Check spam folder. If still missing, try alternate contact method.
