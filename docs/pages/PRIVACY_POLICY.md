# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

ZYRO ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the ZYRO-Electric e-commerce platform (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Platform.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Platform.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number, password, shipping address, billing address
- **Payment Information**: Credit card details, payment method (processed by secure payment gateways)
- **Order Information**: Products purchased, quantities, delivery preferences
- **Communication**: Messages, inquiries, feedback, customer support tickets
- **Profile Information**: Account preferences, wishlist items, saved addresses

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address
- **Usage Information**: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, search queries, product views
- **Location Information**: Geographic location (with your consent)
- **Cookies & Similar Technologies**: Tracking pixels, web beacons, local storage
- **Analytics Data**: Session duration, referral sources, conversion data

### 2.3 Third-Party Information

- **Social Media**: If you link social accounts, we receive profile information
- **Payment Processors**: Transaction and verification data
- **Shipping Partners**: Delivery status and tracking information

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

- **Service Delivery**: Processing orders, managing accounts, customer support
- **Communication**: Order updates, promotional emails (with consent), support responses
- **Analytics**: Understanding user behavior, improving Platform features
- **Personalization**: Recommending products, customizing user experience
- **Security**: Fraud detection, account protection, compliance
- **Marketing**: Targeted advertising, newsletters, special offers (opt-in)
- **Legal Compliance**: Meeting regulatory requirements, preventing illegal activities

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **SSL/TLS Encryption**: Data transmitted over secure HTTPS connection
- **Database Encryption**: Sensitive data encrypted at rest
- **Access Controls**: Limited employee access with authentication
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
- **PCI DSS Compliance**: Payment Card Industry standards for card data
- **GDPR Compliance**: European data protection regulations

### 5.2 Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained while account is active, deleted upon request
- **Transaction Data**: Retained for 7 years (legal/tax requirements)
- **Marketing Data**: Retained until unsubscribe or 2 years of inactivity
- **Support Tickets**: Retained for 3 years for reference
