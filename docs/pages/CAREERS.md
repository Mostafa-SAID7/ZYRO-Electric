## 🎓 Application Process

### Step 1: Submit Application
- Visit careers.zyro-electric.com
- Fill out application
- Upload resume and portfolio (if applicable)

### Step 2: Screening Call
- 30-minute call with recruiter
- Learn more about ZYRO
- Initial qualifications discussion

### Step 3: Technical/Role Assessment
- **Engineering:** Technical coding test
- **Design:** Portfolio review or design challenge
- **Product/Business:** Case study or project
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours

### Step 4: Team Interviews
- Meet with team members
- Discuss projects and experiences
- Learn about team culture
- 2-3 interviews with different team members

### Step 5: Final Interview
- Meet with leadership or hiring manager
- Discuss role expectations
- Answer questions about ZYRO culture
- Discuss compensation and benefits

### Step 6: Offer
- Receive offer letter
- Discuss terms and start date
- Onboarding preparation
