# Shipping Information

**Complete Guide to Shipping & Delivery**

Learn about our shipping options, delivery times, tracking, and policies.

---

## 🚚 Shipping Methods

### Standard Shipping
- **Processing Time:** 2-5 business days
- **Delivery Time:** 5-10 business days
- **Cost:** $5.99 (free over $50 in contiguous US)
- **Best For:** Non-urgent orders, budget-conscious customers
- **Coverage:** Continental US, Alaska, Hawaii

### Expedited Shipping
- **Processing Time:** 1-2 business days
- **Delivery Time:** 2-3 business days
- **Cost:** $14.99 (free for Prime members)
- **Best For:** Time-sensitive orders
- **Coverage:** Continental US only

### Overnight Shipping
- **Processing Time:** Same business day (by 2 PM EST)
- **Delivery Time:** Next business day
- **Cost:** $24.99
- **Best For:** Urgent last-minute orders
- **Coverage:** Continental US only

### Free Shipping
**Qualify for free shipping when:**
- Order total $50+ (Standard, Continental US)
- Prime member (any order, any method)
- Replacement item (defective/wrong)
- Return shipment

## 🎯 Tracking Your Order

### Tracking Number
- **When Provided:** When order ships (Day 5-7)
- **Sent Via:** Email notification
- **Where to View:** Your account dashboard
- **URL Format:** Direct carrier tracking link

### Real-Time Updates

**Track via:**

**1. Email Notification**
- Click "Track Order" in shipping email
- Automatically opens carrier tracking

**2. Your Account**
- Log in to ZYRO account
- Go to "Order History"
- Find order
- Click "Track Shipment"
- Real-time status and location

**3. Carrier Website**
- UPS: www.ups.com (enter tracking #)
- FedEx: www.fedex.com (enter tracking #)
- USPS: www.usps.com (enter tracking #)

### Tracking Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Label Created | Carrier received order |
| In Transit | Package moving toward you |
| Out for Delivery | Package with local carrier |
| Delivered | Package at your address |
| Exception | Delay or issue (contact support) |
| Returned | Package couldn't be delivered |

## 📍 Shipping Address

### Requirements
- Full name required
- Complete street address
- City, state, ZIP code
- Phone number recommended

### Address Issues

**Incomplete Address:**
- May delay processing (1-2 days)
- Contact support with corrections
- May require reshipment

**Correction Window:**
- Can update within 24 hours of order
- Email: support@zyro-electric.com
- Include: Order number, corrected address
- No charge if corrected before shipping

**Post-Shipping Changes:**
- Contact carrier directly
- Carrier may redirect at extra cost
- Or allow delivery and return for free reshipping

### Special Addresses

**Apartment/Suite Numbers:**
- Include in address line 1 or 2
- Example: "123 Main St, Apt 4B"

**Business Addresses:**
- Accepted for delivery
- Signature may be required
- Business hours delivery

**PO Box:**
- Some items ship via USPS only
- PO Boxes acceptable for USPS
- Not acceptable for UPS/FedEx
- We'll use USPS if PO Box selected

**Military Addresses:**
- APO/DPO accepted
- Longer delivery times (14-21 days)
- Select "Military" address type at checkout

## 🚫 Undeliverable Packages

### If Package Can't Be Delivered

**Common Reasons:**
- Address not found
- Recipient not home (multiple attempts)
- Refused by recipient
- Damaged/undeliverable condition

**What Happens:**
1. Carrier attempts delivery 3+ times
2. Returns to our warehouse
3. We contact you by email
4. You have 30 days to redirect

**Options:**
- Correct address, reship (free if address was wrong)
- Reship to alternate address ($9.99)
- Refund original order
- Store credit (110% of amount)

## 🌍 International Shipping

### Customs & Duties

**Import Duties:**
- Recipient responsibility
- Country-dependent
- Not pre-calculated
- Paid at delivery (if required)

**Customs Declaration:**
- Automatically completed by carrier
- Accurate product descriptions
- No false declarations
- May require customs clearance

### Delivery Times
- Processing: 3-5 business days
- Transit: 10-30 business days (varies by country)
- Customs: 2-7 business days additional
- **Total:** 15-42 business days

### Restricted Items
Some items may not ship internationally:
- Batteries (lithium regulations)
- Hazardous materials
- Certain electronics
- Country-specific restrictions

**Check before ordering or contact:** international@zyro-electric.com
