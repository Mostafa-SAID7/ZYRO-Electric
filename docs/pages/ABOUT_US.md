## 🎯 Our Story

ZYRO started with a simple observation: finding the right tech accessories was frustrating. You had to visit dozens of stores or scroll through endless online listings to find what you needed.

In 2024, our founder decided to change that. ZYRO was born—a carefully curated marketplace focused on 9 essential tech niches:

1. **Smartphone Accessories** - Cases, chargers, stands
2. **Laptop Accessories** - Bags, stands, docks
3. **Cable Management** - Organizers and solutions
4. **Desk Tech** - Monitors, keyboards, speakers
5. **Smart Device Accessories** - IoT product add-ons
6. **Charging Solutions** - Fast chargers and power banks
7. **Travel Tech** - Portable and mobile accessories
8. **Photography Accessories** - Tripods, lights, lenses
9. **Content Creator Equipment** - Streaming and recording gear

Today, ZYRO serves thousands of customers worldwide with quality products and exceptional service.
